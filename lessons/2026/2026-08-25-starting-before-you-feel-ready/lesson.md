# Starting Before You Feel Ready

## 原文

Many people wait until they feel fully ready before starting an important task. They want a clear plan, enough time, and the right amount of energy. But these conditions do not always appear. As a result, the task remains unfinished for days or even weeks.

A small start can change this. You do not need to complete the task at once. You can open the document, write one sentence, or spend five minutes collecting ideas. This first step may seem too small to matter, but it reduces the distance between thinking and doing.

Once you begin, the task often becomes clearer. You can see what information is missing and what the next step should be. Starting does not remove every difficulty, but it gives you something real to work with.
