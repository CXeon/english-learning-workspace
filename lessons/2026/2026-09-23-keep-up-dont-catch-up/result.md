## 语言反馈

- 一组介词搭配家族需记完整：`fall behind on` / `catch up on` / `keep up with`，且名词前需要限定词（my plan）。
- `the better choice is to keep up with...`：`choice is` 后接不定式。
- 过去习惯用一般过去时：`In the past, I always tried to...`，不用过去完成进行时；`in past time` 改为 `in the past`。
- 逗号不连接两个完整句子；句中冠词/介词不大写，逗号前不加空格。

## 迁移表达（修订后）

I often fall behind on my learning plan. In the past, I always tried to catch up on everything. But now I understand that the better choice is to keep up with the current plan.
