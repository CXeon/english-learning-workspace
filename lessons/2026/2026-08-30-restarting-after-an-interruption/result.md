## 构思与修订

- 核心观点：写一份简短的 restart note，记录刚完成的事、下一步和待解决问题，并在返回时读取 note、执行下一个行动。
- 内容组织轮中，初稿约 66 词；后补充常见中断来源与重启困难的原因，并将过长句拆分，使全文达到目标长度。
- 语言修订轮优先纠正了 `return to`、`another`、`record what ...` 三个高价值问题。

## 终稿

> After an interruption, it is difficult to return to the original task. A meeting or another task can interrupt your work. When you return to work, you always forget what you finished, and what you should do next. So before switching away from work, write a short restart note to record what you just completed, the next small action, and unresolved questions. When you return to the original task, read the note first, and do the next action written there. This can give you a clear way back into the task, and keep the task moving.
