# Restarting After an Interruption

## 课程目标

- 完成一篇 80–120 词的英文短文，说明如何应对工作中断并顺利重新开始。
- 先构思内容与结构，再完成初稿，最后分两轮修订。
- 在文中自然使用 3–5 个已学词块。

## 写作任务

Write 80–120 words about how to handle an interruption at work and restart smoothly. Explain the problem, describe one or two useful actions, and show how they help you return to the task. Use a general or fictional example; do not include private company or personal information.

## 内容支架

1. **Problem:** What makes it difficult to restart after an interruption?
2. **Method:** What can you write down before switching away, or as soon as the interruption ends?
3. **Return:** What should you do first when you come back?
4. **Result:** How does this habit save time, protect attention, or keep the work moving?

## 可选词块

- write down the next small action
- start without making another decision
- keep the task moving
- return to the original task
- write one or two short lines
- a clear way back into the work
- return your attention to the task

## 课堂任务

1. 不看答案，主动回忆复习队列中当日到期的词块。
2. 确定文章的核心观点、两个主要行动和预期结果。
3. 用英文列出一份简短提纲。
4. 完成 80–120 词初稿，尝试使用 3–5 个已学词块。
5. 第一轮修订：只检查内容是否完整、顺序是否清楚。
6. 第二轮修订：只检查语言，优先修正 2–3 个高价值问题。
