## 理解与词块

- 主旨概括抓住核心“及时同步问题与进展，给对方留出反应时间”；动词误用 notice → share ... with / report ... to。
- 理解题一：周五延迟变失败的原因起初只答到 "removed their choices"，补全时间对比（周二尚有求助、上报、缩范围等选项）后理解到位；"choices of teammates" 属搭配错位。
- 理解题二：close the loop / open loops 释义与效果完全正确。
- `let go of the idea that` 首次无提示补全整体框架，但 that 从句内缺主语（that buy → that you need a new computer），从句语法仍需验证。
- `non-negotiable` 词性与用法正确，词义仍偏“不可回避”，精确义“不可协商、不容更改”待巩固。
- `a clear follow-up question` 词义正确，搭配 save your time → save you time；`unlock the next step` 词义正确，句中多了 and（When ..., and ...）。
- `narrow the question down` 说出的是效果（“更容易回答”），精确义“缩小范围、聚焦到具体一点”待巩固。
- 新学五个词块：keep someone informed、a quick status update、flag a delay early、close the loop、no surprises。

## 迁移表达

> Hello everyone, I can't complete the task of hospital on Friday. Because some details are complex, I flag a delay early. I will finish it when next Monday, no surprises.

反馈要点：the task of hospital → the hospital task；when next Monday → by next Monday；I flag → I want to flag（消息语境）；主动使用了 flag a delay early 与 no surprises。

自然版本："I want to flag a delay early: the hospital task will not be ready by Friday, because some details are more complex than expected. I will finish it by next Monday and share a quick status update tomorrow, so there are no surprises."
