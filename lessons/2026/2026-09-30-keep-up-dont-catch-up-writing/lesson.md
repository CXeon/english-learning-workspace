# Keep Up, Don't Catch Up: Writing

## 课程目标

- 完成一篇 80–110 词的英文短文，说明学习计划落后时为什么应该跟上当前计划，而不是补齐所有遗漏。
- 先完成内容构思和英文提纲，再写初稿，最后分两轮修订。
- 自然使用至少三个本课目标词块。

## 写作任务

Write 80–110 words about why learners should keep up with the current plan instead of trying to complete every missed lesson. Explain what can happen when a learner falls behind, what they can do on a busy day, and how this choice keeps learning continuous. Use a general or fictional example; do not include private company or personal information.

## 内容支架

1. **Situation:** How can a learner fall behind on a plan?
2. **Problem:** Why does trying to catch up on everything make learning feel heavier?
3. **Choice:** What should the learner do instead?
4. **Busy-day action:** What could a lighter version of the lesson look like?
5. **Result:** How does this choice protect continuity?

## 本课目标词块

- fall behind on something
- catch up on something
- keep up with the current plan
- a lighter version of
- let go of the idea that

## 课堂任务

1. 不看答案，主动回忆五个目标词块。
2. 确定文章的核心观点、忙碌时的行动与预期结果。
3. 用英文列出一份简短提纲。
4. 完成 80–110 词初稿，自然使用至少三个目标词块。
5. 第一轮修订：只检查内容是否完整、顺序是否清楚。
6. 第二轮修订：只检查语言，优先修正 2–3 个高价值问题。
