# 课程结果

## 完成情况

- 课程于 2026-10-03 开始、2026-10-06 跨日完成，是一节按 45 分钟结构执行的非音频阅读课。
- 完成五个旧词块主动回忆、计时阅读、词汇讲解、主旨概括、关键细节定位、两道理解题、五个新词块学习、简短复述与四句迁移表达。
- 计时阅读约 402 词，用时 6 分 16 秒。首次阅读因把 5 分钟误解为硬性时限而加速；不限时重读首段后恢复了因果理解。

## 理解与词块

- 能抓住文章核心：遇到不明确的请求时，应提出具体、容易回答的追问，并在得到回答后确认理解。
- 学习了 `a clear follow-up question`、`narrow the question down`、`unlock the next step`、`check your understanding`、`send the work in the wrong direction`。
- `choices` 首次写成动词 `chooses`，提示词性后能在完整句中正确使用。
- `an unclear request` 首次写成 `a unclear request`，并使用了不完整的 `when receiving ...`；反馈后能写出完整从句 `when we receive an unclear request`。
- `prevent someone from doing something` 首次把 `from` 拼成 `form`，指出后能立即修正。

## 简短复述

> Clear follow-up questions help us to check our understanding.
>
> We can make a follow-up question easier to answer by giving the other person two clear choices.
>
> We should ask a follow-up question when we receive an unclear request.
>
> After we receive an answer, we should check our understanding.

## 迁移表达

> The word “improve” is unclear.
>
> Do you want me to scale the report down or add more detail?
>
> So I'll shorten the report and remove unnecessary details. Is that right?
>
> This clear follow-up question can prevent me from sending the work in the wrong direction.
