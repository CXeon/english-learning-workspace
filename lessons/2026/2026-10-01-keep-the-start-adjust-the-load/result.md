## 词块回忆

- 4/5 首次回忆完整正确：`fall behind on something`、`catch up on something`、`keep up with the current plan`、`a lighter version of`。
- `let go of the idea that` 回忆为 "give up the idea about must..."：意思方向对，但词块本体（let go of）与 that + 完整句子结构均需巩固，下次复习最优先。

## 阅读与理解

- 阅读后主动列出 8 个不确定表达并全部当场讲解：drifting away / motivation / cooperates / separate / intensity / adjusted / shows up / matter less；其中 intensity、show up 与本课新词块重合。
- 主旨概括抓住开头对比（not motivation but energy），但漏掉全文核心"固定与可变"的划分，经反馈给出自然版本。
- 理解题第 1 题：两个错误复述准确，"共同点"一问未答，经提示补上（都是 all-or-nothing，忽略精力每天不同）。
- 理解题第 2 题答偏：用核心任务（core task）的句子回答固定开始时间的作用；正确答案为"时间即信号，免去重新决策"。

## 语言反馈

- choice（名词）与 choose（动词）区分：I will choose a fixed starting time。
- 少了 as non-negotiable 会改变句意：treating a full lesson as non-negotiable（把完整课当成不容商量的规矩）。
- skip the lesson：单数可数名词前需要定冠词。
- the idea that + 完整句子，不用 about。

## 迁移表达（修订后）

I will choose a fixed starting time. When the clock reaches that point, learning begins. On a tired day, I will scale the lesson down to its core instead of skipping it.

## 新学词块

- a fixed starting time
- non-negotiable
- match the intensity to your energy
- scale something down
- show up anyway
