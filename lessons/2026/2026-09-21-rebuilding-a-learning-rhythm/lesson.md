# Rebuilding a Learning Rhythm

## 课程目标

- 完成一篇 80–110 词的英文短文，说明忙碌一段时间后如何重建学习节奏。
- 先构思内容并列英文提纲，再完成初稿，最后分两轮修订。
- 在文中自然使用 3–5 个已学词块。

## 写作任务

Write 80–110 words about how to rebuild your learning rhythm after a busy period. Explain why you should not double your workload to make up for lost time, how you can pick up where you left off, and how a small daily minimum can help you become consistent again. Use a general or fictional example; do not include private company or personal information.

## 内容支架

1. **Situation:** What happened during the busy period?
2. **Decision:** Why is doubling the missed work unhelpful?
3. **Restart:** Where should the learner begin again?
4. **Daily action:** What small daily minimum is realistic?
5. **Result:** How will this rebuild the rhythm and protect earlier progress?

## 本课目标词块

- make up for lost time
- pick up where you left off
- a small daily minimum
- rebuild the rhythm
- protect the progress you have already made

## 课堂任务

1. 不看答案，主动回忆复习队列中到期的五个词块。
2. 确定文章的核心观点、三个主要行动与预期结果。
3. 用英文列出一份简短提纲。
4. 完成 80–110 词初稿，尝试自然使用 3–5 个目标词块。
5. 第一轮修订：只检查内容是否完整、顺序是否清楚。
6. 第二轮修订：只检查语言，优先修正 2–3 个高价值问题。
