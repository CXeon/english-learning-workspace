## 到期词块回忆

- 首次准确回忆 `a small daily minimum`。
- `rebuild the rhythm` 和 `protect the progress you have already made` 的意思回忆正确，但首次尝试存在冠词或表达形式偏差。
- `make up for lost time` 首次误作 `find the lost time`，第二次漏掉 `for`，经反馈后在终稿中准确使用。
- `pick up where you left off` 首次未能回忆，经反馈后能准确回忆并用于终稿。

## 构思与修订

- 内容按“人的精力有限 → 不用双倍任务补进度 → 从停下的地方继续 → 设定每日最低量 → 保护已有进步”展开。
- 内容修订轮删除了重复结论，并把“从哪里继续”与“如何降低每日负担”分开表达。
- 语言修订轮优先修正了 `do twice as much work`、`while also keeping up with their current plan`、`set a small daily minimum` 及 `an easier version of the plan`。

## 终稿

> People have limited energy, so they usually find it difficult to do twice as much work while also keeping up with their current plan. So instead of trying to make up for lost time, they should pick up where they left off and set a small daily minimum to rebuild the rhythm.
>
> They can base the daily minimum on the original plan, and choose a few easy tasks or follow an easier version of the plan.
>
> If they do it, they will protect the progress they have already made.
