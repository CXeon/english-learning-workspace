# Before You Assume, Ask: Writing

## 课程目标

- 完成一篇 80–100 词的英文短文，用通用或虚构的协作场景说明：如何识别不明确之处、提出包含具体选项的追问、确认理解并避免返工。
- 先完成内容构思和英文提纲，再写初稿，最后分两轮修订。
- 自然使用至少两个本课目标词块，优先 `narrow the question down` 和 `unlock the next step`。

## 写作任务

Write 80–100 words about how to handle an unclear request in a collaboration setting. Use a general or fictional scenario. Explain how to notice what is unclear, ask a follow-up question with specific choices, check your understanding, and avoid rework. Use at least two of the target chunks. Do not include private company or personal information.

## 内容支架

1. **Situation:** What short request from a teammate was unclear?
2. **Unclear point:** Which word or detail could be understood in more than one way?
3. **Follow-up:** What question with specific choices can you ask to narrow it down?
4. **Confirmation:** How do you check your understanding after the answer?
5. **Result:** How does this short question prevent rework?

## 本课目标词块

- a clear follow-up question
- narrow the question down
- unlock the next step
- check your understanding
- send the work in the wrong direction

## 到期复习词块

- narrow the question down（优先）
- unlock the next step（优先）
- let go of the idea that（优先）
- non-negotiable
- match the intensity to your energy

## 课堂任务

1. 不看答案，主动回忆上面五个到期词块，优先补全 `narrow the question down` 和 `unlock the next step`。
2. 构思：确定场景、不明确之处、追问方式、确认方式与避免返工的结果。
3. 用英文列出一份简短提纲。
4. 完成 80–100 词初稿，自然使用至少两个本课目标词块。
5. 第一轮修订：只检查内容是否完整、顺序是否清楚。
6. 第二轮修订：只检查语言，优先修正 2–3 个高价值问题。
