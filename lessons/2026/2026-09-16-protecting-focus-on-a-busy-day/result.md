## 词块回忆

- 首次回忆时，四个词块的意思都能表达出来，但有 `focused`、`can wait`、冠词 `the` 和 `at once` 的形式偏差。
- 经一轮反馈后，准确回忆了 `protect a block of focused time`、`decide what can wait`、`return to the original task` 和 `do not need to complete the task at once`。
- 写作中成功复用了其中三个词块。

## 语言反馈

- 日常做法宜保持现在时：`When I am interrupted ...`。
- 继续巩固冠词与搭配：`an urgent task` 和 `focus on completing ...`。
- 情态动词后用动词原形：`must read`，不是 `must to read`。
- 主语 `This` 后用 `makes`；多个完整句不用逗号直接连接。

## 终稿

I often have many tasks on a busy workday, and every task seems urgent. I do not need to complete every task at once, so I identify the most important one and protect a block of focused time to work on it. When I am interrupted by an urgent task, I write a simple note to record what I have just completed and what I need to do next. Then I focus on completing the urgent task. When I return to the original task, I read the note first. This makes me feel clear and productive, and I can complete my tasks one at a time.
