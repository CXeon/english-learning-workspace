## 构思与修订

- 内容按“学习者精力有限 → 保留固定开始时间和核心任务 → 低精力时缩小课程 → 维持计划连续”展开。
- 内容修订轮补充了低精力时的具体做法：把课程缩小到核心任务，并明确是学习者 `show up anyway`。
- 语言修订轮重点处理了不可数名词 `energy` 的主谓一致、`after + -ing`、`stopping` 的拼写、`stop work for the day` 的语义、`feel like a failure` 以及 `every day` / `everyday` 的区分。

## 终稿

> Many learners' energy is limited. They often fall behind because they start studying after stopping work for the day and spending time with their family. To keep up with their learning plan without feeling like failures, they can choose a fixed starting time every day and make one core task non-negotiable. They can match the intensity to their energy. On a low-energy day, scale the lesson down to the core task. If learners show up anyway, they keep the plan alive and don't feel like failures.
