# Keep the Start, Adjust the Load: Writing

## 课程目标

- 完成一篇 80–110 词的英文短文，说明如何根据当天精力调整学习强度，同时保留固定开始时间和核心任务。
- 先完成内容构思和英文提纲，再写初稿，最后分两轮修订。
- 自然使用至少三个本课目标词块。

## 写作任务

Write 80–110 words about how a learner can adjust the intensity of a lesson to match their energy while keeping a fixed starting time and one core task. Explain what should remain non-negotiable, what can be scaled down on a low-energy day, and why the learner should show up anyway. Use a general or fictional example; do not include private company or personal information.

## 内容支架

1. **Situation:** How can a learner's energy change from day to day?
2. **Fixed point:** What starting time should stay fixed, and why?
3. **Core task:** What small task should remain non-negotiable?
4. **Adjustment:** How can the learner scale the lesson down on a low-energy day?
5. **Result:** Why is it useful to show up anyway?

## 本课目标词块

- a fixed starting time
- non-negotiable
- match the intensity to your energy
- scale something down
- show up anyway

## 重点复查

- let go of the idea that + 完整句子

## 课堂任务

1. 不看答案，主动回忆 2026-10-03 到期的五个目标词块，并重点补全 `let go of the idea that` 的结构。
2. 确定文章的核心观点、固定项、可调整项与预期结果。
3. 用英文列出一份简短提纲。
4. 完成 80–110 词初稿，自然使用至少三个目标词块。
5. 第一轮修订：只检查内容是否完整、顺序是否清楚。
6. 第二轮修订：只检查语言，优先修正 2–3 个高价值问题。
